# Optimus-Elite Superstore Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes four years of sales data from Optimus-Elite Superstore to identify key drivers of revenue, profit, regional performance, customer segment contribution, product performance, and growth opportunities.

The goal was to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights using Power BI.

## Business Questions

- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which products contribute the least profit?
- Which regions and regional managers perform best?
- Which states contribute the highest and lowest profit?
- Which customer segments contribute most to profit?
- Which order priority levels are most profitable?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which months and years show the strongest performance?

## Key Insights

- Total revenue reached **₦14.21M**, while total profit reached **₦13.87M**.
- The business sold **6,033 units across 54 products**.
- Revenue grew consistently from 2011 to 2014, increasing by approximately **103%**.
- **Phones and Chairs together generated 63% of total profit**.
- Phones were the strongest-performing sub-category, generating approximately **₦5.35M in profit**.
- The **North West region**, led by Zainab Abubakar, generated the highest regional profit at approximately **₦2.72M**.
- The **South East region** recorded the lowest regional profit at approximately **₦1.71M**.
- The Consumer segment contributed approximately **49% of total profit**, ahead of Corporate and Home Office.
- Medium and High priority orders generated the majority of profit, while Low-priority orders contributed only about **₦0.59M**.
- Yobe was the strongest state by profit, while Bauchi ranked lowest among the states analyzed.

## Recommendations

- Review pricing and cost structures for the lowest-profit products.
- Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing categories such as Phones and Chairs.
- Study the North West region's sales strategy and apply successful practices to weaker regions.
- Investigate why Low-priority orders generate significantly less profit.
- Replicate the conditions that contributed to the strongest-performing months.
- Focus on strengthening high-value customer segments and underperforming states.
